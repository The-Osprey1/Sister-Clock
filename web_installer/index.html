SisterClock ✨

Custom ESP8266 + MAX7219 Dot Matrix Clock
Target Hardware: ESP8266 Wemos D1 Mini
Display: 4-in-1 MAX7219 8x32 LED Matrix (FC-16)
3D Enclosure: MakerWorld Project 3009244
Pins: CLK → D5 (14), CS → D7 (13), DIN → D8 (15)

Plug your ESP8266 D1 Mini into your computer via USB (in Chrome or Edge), then click below:

  1. Click Connect & Flash and select your D1 Mini COM port.
  2. Once flashing finishes, connect your phone to Wi-Fi SisterClock-Setup using password bestbrother.
  3. Open http://192.168.4.1, choose your home Wi-Fi, and check your time zone.
