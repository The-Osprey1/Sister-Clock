# Your SisterClock 💛

## Get started

1. Plug in the clock and give it a moment to start.
2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network **SisterClock-Setup**. The password is **bestbrother**. Your phone may say this network has no internet; stay connected to it.
3. Open a browser and go to **http://192.168.4.1**.
4. Choose your home Wi-Fi, enter its password, and check your time zone. The clock needs a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
5. Tap **Connect clock**. Keep it plugged in while it restarts. Reconnect your phone to your home Wi-Fi.
6. Watch the clock for a number like `192.168.1.123`. Enter that number in your browser to open the controls. You can also try **http://sisterclock.local**. Save the page as a bookmark or add it to your phone’s home screen using your browser’s share or menu button.

If the clock returns to setup, reconnect to **SisterClock-Setup** and check the network name and password. It can take a couple of minutes for connection attempts to finish.

## Everyday use

Open your saved clock page while your phone is on the same home Wi-Fi. Use **Adjust brightness and appearance** to make the display comfortable. Tap **Set an alarm**, turn the alarm on, choose a time and days, and tap **Save alarms**. Use **Test Alarm** to preview it. Sound requires a connected, enabled buzzer.

Use the message box to send a note to the clock. Stop and snooze controls are in Quick Actions. Nighttime dimming is available under Display settings.

## If something goes wrong

- Check the clock has power and your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the clock.
- If your router or power goes off, leave the clock plugged in; it will try to reconnect.
- If you changed your Wi-Fi name or password, unplug the clock and plug it back in. Allow a few minutes for it to return to **SisterClock-Setup**, then join that network and repeat setup.
- If the saved page does not open, try **http://sisterclock.local** or the address shown on the clock after it starts.
